Research Interests & Vision

I build self-improving AI agents that reason reliably over temporal, multimodal, and physical-world data, advancing agent-based task automation toward human–agent collaboration, and exploring new frontiers of perception in autonomous agents operating in complex, real-world environments.

But naive scaling backfires. More Modalities, More Problems: debate can cost ≈22× the tokens and 12× the time, with 74% of errors from a shared modality bias. Visual Anchoring (closed-form drift bound) and MacroLens (4,416-equity, leakage-controlled benchmark) add the theory and evaluation.

Future Research

One program, pursued along three directions, one per pillar.

DIRECTION 1 · AUTONOMY

Self-Improving Agentic Systems

Agents that synthesize, verify, and reuse their own tools and workflows, so competence compounds across tasks.

Near-term: open-source self-improving AutoML agent, plus a workflow-prediction benchmark.

DIRECTION 2 · RELIABILITY

Verification, Not Persuasion

Reasoning adjudicated by programmatic verification, calibration, and theory-backed control.

Near-term: verification-grounded reasoning library, plus an auditability & calibration suite.

DIRECTION 3 · PROACTIVENESS

Proactive, Ambient Agency

Always-on agents that decide what to investigate and act on their own initiative under human-set policies.

Near-term: an ambient multi-agent system, evaluated by archival replay.
